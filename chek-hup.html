The Overview

Where tradition and taste meet.

Chek Hup is an Ipoh institution known for its 3 in 1 heritage milk tea. For February 2025, the brief was to deepen the brand's place in the Malay market. I built a campaign that paired Chek Hup Teh Tarik with the dishes Malaysians grew up on, framing every cup as part of a shared cultural moment.

Campaign · February 2025

Heritage Stories, More Than a Sip

Every post paired a cup of Chek Hup Teh Tarik with an icon of Malaysian food culture, from nasi lemak and kuih to laksa and satay, plus warm Ramadan and family moments. The tone leaned into heritage, warmth, and that first familiar sip, all in a Malay first voice.
