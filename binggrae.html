The Overview

A K brand that needed to feel local, loud, and loved.

Binggrae is huge in Korea, but in Malaysia the job is to keep Melona and Samanco top of mind month after month. I run the monthly content engine on our IOO framework and lead the bigger moments, from a live concert activation to seasonal campaigns, each one designed to ride the K wave and build real engagement.

Campaign 01 · January 2026

The Sweetest Setlist

For the QWER 1st World Tour Rockation in Kuala Lumpur, Binggrae ran a full concert activation. I led the content arc across the whole event: a teaser to build hype, a cover reveal, an announcement for a 2000 ice cream giveaway, live booth coverage, and post event storytelling, all wrapped in a playful K pop energy.

Campaign 02 · June 2026

Sunny Skies, Binggrae Vibes

A summer campaign built around one line: cool in every frame, iconic in every bite. The push mixed bold product hero shots, lifestyle scenes, a find and win maze contest, and winner reveals, keeping Melona and Samanco in the feed all season and rewarding the community that showed up.

Binggrae Picnic Club
