A little about me

I'm a marketer who fell for the craft.

I've spent the last decade moving between retail floors, operations, and content studios, and every stop taught me the same thing: people don't buy features, they buy feeling.

Early on, I grew a TikTok account from 9,000 to 500,000 followers in seven months and racked up over 20 million views. These days I lead content strategy at Newnormz, where I've helped secure over RM1.3M in client projects and quietly rebuilt how the team works with AI.

I run hot on ideas and energy. I'd rather ship ten bold things and learn than polish one safe one forever. If that sounds like your kind of trouble, we'll get along.
