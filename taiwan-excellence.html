The Overview

Make them laugh, then make them care.

Taiwan Excellence is the national mark of innovation, spotlighting the best Taiwanese brands. I contribute to the long running Malaysia account. For July 2026 the theme was Go Healthy and Accessibility with Taiwan Innovation, told mostly through memes, because the fastest way to make people care about a tyre or a fitness machine is to make them smile first.

Campaign · July 2026

Go Healthy with Taiwan

The campaign leaned into relatable, scroll stopping memes for products like the Maxxis HP6 Premitra tyre and Johnson fitness gear, plus an idea competition worth USD30,000 for the next big health innovation. Smart tech and real accessibility, wrapped in humour that travels.
